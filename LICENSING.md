| Original author-controlled documentation, synthetic demonstrations and releasable aggregate results | CC BY 4.0 (`LICENSE-CONTENT.md`) | Verify permissions and any research-partner/institutional restrictions for original-study materials before publication |
| Third-party Python dependencies | Their own upstream licenses | Installing dependencies does not incorporate or relicense them |
| 25-row synthetic examples | CC BY 4.0 | Not survey respondents and not evidence of empirical model validity |
| Confidential household survey records, individual predictions and private scientific logs | **Excluded** | Not present and must never be committed |
| `CITATION.cff` | Preferred scholarly attribution | The paper DOI is not a software DOI |
